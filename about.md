This public version contains synthetic placeholder material so the typography,
reading width, mathematics, tables, figures, and interactions can be reviewed
without publishing unfinished work.

The intended design is quiet and reader-oriented. Mathematics appears when it
compresses an argument, while interaction is reserved for quantities that are
useful to vary or inspect.

### Design principles

- **Readable prose** uses a stable measure and restrained hierarchy.
- **Mathematical blocks** remain legible on narrow screens.
- **Figures** share the typography and visual rhythm of the article.
- **Interactive elements** should add explanatory value.

### Colophon

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and
[MathJax](https://www.mathjax.org/), with Plotly loaded only for figures that
need interaction.
